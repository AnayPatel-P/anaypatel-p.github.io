Scope: the whole single-page portfolio (index.html). Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: MLE/DS/AI recruiters (30-second scan, often on a phone) and engineers (3-5 minutes). Action: open the resume or get in touch. Proof: three live projects, six skills, Wio Bank and Estée Lauder experience. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages; light and dark; 360px to 1440px; WCAG 2.2 AA; Lighthouse 100; reduced motion honored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a curated collection in a museum specimen cabinet. A nameplate introduces Anay; everything else lives in labelled drawers you pull open, each holding real specimens. It refuses the hero-over-card-grid portfolio and the scrolling stack of numbered sections.

OWN-WORLD: Painted steel cabinet: pale sage-putty steel in light, deep collection green in dark. Brass label holders framing typed paper labels (Courier Prime, data only); brass cup pulls; drawer trays with real depth shadow; specimens on cool white paper cards with typed data strips. Display and body in Bricolage Grotesque. Restrained colour: steel owns the ground, brass is the single accent, paper islands carry content.

STORY: The visitor learns who Anay is from the nameplate in one glance, sees three live products already laid out in the open Shipped drawer, and can pull Toolkit, Field record and Correspondence for depth. Closed drawers still scan: each label states its contents. They leave with the resume or an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 40% is a sticky nameplate, name at display scale, role line, one-liner, Resume as the brass primary action, GitHub, LinkedIn and Email, then a drawer index. Right 60% is the cabinet: Shipped drawer open with three specimens, three labelled drawers closed beneath. Phone: nameplate, then the cabinet full width.

FORM: Collection cabinet, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:29d2b4adcc42dcf6. Signature interaction: pulling a drawer, instant press response, interruptible slide, cross-drawer links open the target drawer and light the specimen. Motion grammar: ease-out 380ms opening, 260ms closing, transform and opacity plus grid-row reveal; one load moment, the open drawer's specimens settling in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
